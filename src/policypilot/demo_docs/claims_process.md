# Sample Policy: How to File a Claim

This fictional guide is part of the PolicyPilot demo documents.

## Reporting an accident

Report an accident or a theft to the insurer within 30 days. Theft must also be reported to the police within 48 hours, and the police report number must be included with the claim. Late reporting can reduce or void the payment if the delay makes it harder to investigate the loss.

## Documents needed

To file a claim, send the claim form, photos of the damage, the other driver's details, the police report number if there is one, and repair estimates. For an injury claim, include the medical bills.

## Claim decision and payment

The insurer acknowledges a claim within 5 business days and makes a decision within 30 days after receiving all documents. Approved claims are paid within 10 business days. If the policyholder disagrees with the decision, they can ask for an internal review within 60 days.

## Effect on the premium

An at-fault claim can increase the premium at renewal. Motor vehicle record points from traffic violations can also increase the premium. Claims where the policyholder was not at fault do not change the premium.
