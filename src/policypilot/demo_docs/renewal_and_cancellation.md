# Sample Policy: Renewal, Payment and Cancellation

This fictional document is part of the PolicyPilot demo documents.

## Grace period for payment

If a premium payment is missed, there is a grace period of 15 days. Coverage continues during the grace period. If the premium is still unpaid after the grace period, the policy is cancelled and coverage ends.

## Reinstatement

A cancelled policy can be reinstated within 30 days after cancellation if all unpaid premium is paid and there were no claims while the policy was cancelled. After 30 days, a new application is required.

## Cancelling the policy

The policyholder can cancel at any time by written notice. The unused part of the premium is refunded pro rata, minus an administration fee of 25 dollars. The insurer can cancel for non-payment or for fraud, with 10 days' written notice.

## Renewal

The policy renews automatically every 12 months. The renewal premium is sent at least 30 days before the renewal date and takes into account claims, motor vehicle record points and changes in the vehicle's use.
