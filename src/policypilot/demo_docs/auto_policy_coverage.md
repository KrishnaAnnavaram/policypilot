# Sample Personal Auto Policy: Coverage Summary

This is a fictional sample policy written for the PolicyPilot demo. It is not a real insurance contract.

## Liability coverage

Liability coverage pays for bodily injury and property damage that the insured driver causes to other people. It does not pay for damage to the insured vehicle itself. The liability limit shown on the declarations page is the most the insurer will pay for any one accident.

## Collision and comprehensive coverage

Collision coverage pays to repair the insured vehicle after a crash with another vehicle or an object, minus the deductible. Comprehensive coverage pays for theft, fire, hail, flood, vandalism and hitting an animal, also minus the deductible. Both coverages pay at most the actual cash value of the vehicle at the time of the loss.

## Deductibles

The deductible is the amount the policyholder pays on each collision or comprehensive claim before the insurer pays. The standard deductible is 500 dollars. A higher deductible of 1,000 dollars lowers the premium. No deductible applies to liability claims.

## Exclusions

The policy does not cover a vehicle used to carry passengers or goods for a fee unless commercial use is declared and the commercial-use premium is paid. Damage caused intentionally, damage while racing, normal wear and tear, and mechanical breakdown are excluded. A driver whose licence is suspended or revoked is not covered while driving.

## Commercial use

A vehicle that is used mainly for business, such as deliveries or visiting clients, must be declared as commercial use. If commercial use is not declared, a claim that happens during business use may be refused.
